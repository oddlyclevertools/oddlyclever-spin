Your music.
On your device.

I built Spin, a free music player for Windows and Android. Search, play and keep songs, with no account, no ads and no servers of mine in between.

Windows 10 and 11 Android 7 and up iPhone later

Free and open source. It isn't in the app stores, so you install the file yourself: the FAQ walks you through it.

What it does

One search for everything

Your own songs and new music in one box, with albums, artists and the best match on top as you type.

Play straight away

Press any song and it plays. Keep the ones you love with proper titles, artists, albums and square covers.

Your library, your files

Songs are normal music files in your Music folder. Other players see them, and uninstalling never touches them.

19 looks

Classic themes and palette themes, light and dark, or make your own from a photo or the cover that's playing.

Live backgrounds

Calm drawn landscapes that take on the colours of your theme and move gently behind your music.

Lyrics, queue, sleep timer

Synced lyrics, Up next you can drag to reorder, shuffle, repeat, and a sleep timer that fades out.

How it works

You search

Spin looks the song up in YouTube Music's catalogue, straight from your own device.

It plays or saves

It streams the song to you, or saves it as a music file with its cover and song info.

Nothing in between

There are no servers of mine and no account. I never see what you search, play or save.

Please download only music you have the right to

Spin is for your personal library: music you've bought, music that's free or Creative Commons, or music the artist lets you save. Respect artists and the terms of the services you use.

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC" or "unknown publisher"

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen doesn't know it. Click More info, then Run anyway. It installs just for you, so it doesn't ask for admin rights.

You can check the file first: each release lists its SHA-256 checksum in SHA256SUMS.txt, and PowerShell shows yours with Get-FileHash .\OddlyCleverSpin-Setup-*.exe.

How do I install the Android app (APK)?
  1. Tap Android above to download the APK.
  2. Open it from your notifications or the Files app. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. To update later, install the new APK over the old one. Your songs and settings stay.
Can my phone update it by itself? (Obtainium)

Obtainium is a free Android app that installs apps from their release pages and keeps them updated.

  1. Install Obtainium.
  2. On your phone, tap Add Spin to Obtainium. Or open Obtainium, tap Add app and paste https://github.com/oddlyclevertools/oddlyclever-spin.
  3. Tap Add, then Install. Obtainium tells you when a new version is out.
Where are my songs saved?

Windows: in Music\OddlyClever Music in your user folder.

Android: in your phone's Music folder, so they show in Files and other music players.

Uninstalling Spin never deletes your songs.

Is it legal?

Spin is a player for your personal library, and how you use it is up to you. Saving music you've bought, free and Creative Commons music, or music the artist allows is fine. Most services' terms don't allow downloading everything else, so please download only music you have the right to, for your own personal use. Spin is not made by or connected to YouTube or Google.

What about iPhone, Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Windows and Android come first. An iPhone version needs a computer and weekly re-signing to install, so I'm keeping it for later.

Does it collect anything?

No. No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash tracking. Read the privacy page for exactly what it talks to and when.

Downloads stopped working

YouTube changes things every few weeks. Spin updates its download engine by itself; if it still fails, update to the newest release. Something else broken? Tell me on GitHub Issues.
